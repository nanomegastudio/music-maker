🥁 Drum sounds

Untick a sound and the generator stops using it: Generate new song, 🎲 on a drum track and ↻ on a section all leave it out. Unticking also takes it out of this song's drum tracks (Ctrl+Z undoes that). Click a name to hear it. The choice is saved with the project and carries over to new songs.
🎚 Equalizer

Shapes the sound of the whole mix as it plays, live. It applies to the sampled and built-in synth sounds; MIDI devices and the exported .mid file are not changed. Saved with the project.
📁 Projects

Projects are saved inside this browser on this PC. A save keeps everything exactly as it is: every track and note, the sections, the generator settings (style, key, phrase, melody leaps), the view and the undo history. Each save also keeps a version, so you can go back to an earlier state.
